# Content Plan — Whimsical Moss

All copy in this file is draft copy. The owner should approve or edit it before launch. Do not present unconfirmed claims as facts.

## 1. Brand positioning

**Working positioning:** Handmade creations, thoughtfully made around your ideas.

**Core message:** Explore handmade crafts, calligraphy, painting, and custom pieces. Bring an idea; the maker will discuss how to turn it into a creation.

**Tone:** Personal, imaginative, clear, warm, and concise. Avoid exaggerated claims and generic phrases in every section.

## 4. Content rules

- No fake testimonials, ratings, sales figures, awards, or customer logos.
- No claims about shipping, turnaround, payment, or returns until confirmed.
- Avoid claiming every idea can be fulfilled; the maker should confirm feasibility.
- Use actual product names where supplied.
- Do not call an item available for purchase unless the owner confirms availability.
- Make it clear that enquiries are not confirmed orders.
