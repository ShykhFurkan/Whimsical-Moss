## Stage 1 defaults unless changed
- Static-first Next.js landing page
- No database, CMS, accounts, checkout, or payment processing
- WhatsApp is the primary enquiry route
- Instagram is the secondary route
- Real artwork photography is required for production
- Motion is subtle and respects reduced-motion preferences
